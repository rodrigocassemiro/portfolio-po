# Rodrigo Cassemiro — Product Owner Portfolio

Product strategy, discovery, roadmaps, prioritized backlogs, user stories and responsible AI cases focused on measurable outcomes.

## What This Portfolio Demonstrates

This is a Product Owner portfolio rather than a software-code portfolio. It shows how I:

- Frame business and user problems before selecting solutions
- Translate product vision into outcomes, roadmaps and MVP boundaries
- Prioritize backlogs using value, risk, evidence and dependencies
- Align business, operations, engineering, compliance and customer needs
- Define success metrics and guardrails
- Document trade-offs and revisit conditions
- Apply AI and automation with human oversight and privacy-by-design

## Working Principles

1. Outcomes before outputs
2. Problems before solutions
3. Evidence before certainty
4. Small, testable releases before large bets
5. Human accountability for consequential AI decisions
6. Clear trade-offs and explicit revisit conditions
