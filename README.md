# Product Owner Portfolio

A collection of product management case studies, roadmaps, user stories and AI-driven product initiatives.

## About

This repository showcases practical examples of Product Management, Product Ownership and Digital Transformation projects focused on delivering measurable business outcomes.

## Featured Case Studies

### AI-Assisted Call Quality & Compliance Platform

An anonymized conceptual product initiative that uses secure speech transcription and explainable AI signals to help Quality Assurance teams identify potential compliance, accuracy and knowledge-base issues faster, while preserving human review.

Key Areas:

- AI Product Strategy
- Speech-to-Text and Language Analysis
- Quality Assurance Workflows
- Human-in-the-Loop Review
- Responsible AI and Privacy
- Compliance Rule Management
- Product Discovery and Governance

[View Case Study](case-studies/ai-call-quality-compliance-platform.md) · [View Roadmap](roadmaps/ai-call-quality-compliance-roadmap.md)

### AI-Powered Real Estate Marketplace

An end-to-end marketplace initiative focused on improving the rental experience through automation, AI integration and customer-centric product design.

Key Areas:

- Product Discovery
- Roadmap Definition
- User Stories
- Process Automation
- Customer Journey Design
- Stakeholder Alignment

[View Case Study](case-studies/AI-Powered-Real-Estate-Marketplace.md) · [View Roadmap](roadmaps/marketplace-roadmap.md)
