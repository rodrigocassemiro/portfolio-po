# AI Call Quality & Compliance Product Roadmap

## Vision

Help Quality Assurance teams review authorized customer-service calls faster and more consistently through secure transcription, explainable risk signals and human validation.

## Phase 1 – Discovery, Governance & Baseline

- Map the current QA workflow and review bottlenecks
- Define permitted use cases and prohibited automated decisions
- Establish review-time, coverage and accuracy baselines
- Identify countries, languages and regulatory constraints
- Complete privacy, security, legal and employee-representation assessments
- Define the rule-library ownership model

### Outcome

A validated problem, agreed guardrails and measurable product outcomes.

---

## Phase 2 – Secure Transcription Foundation

- Approved call-recording ingestion
- Speech-to-text transcription
- Speaker diarization and timestamps
- Sensitive-data masking
- Role-based access control
- Retention and deletion controls
- Audit logging

### Outcome

Authorized reviewers can securely locate and read relevant call moments.

---

## Phase 3 – AI-Assisted Quality Review MVP

- Configurable compliance and quality rules
- Evidence-linked transcript flags
- Confidence indicators
- QA review queue
- Confirm, reject and annotate actions
- Versioned reviewer decisions
- Basic dashboard

### Outcome

QA analysts can prioritize calls and validate potential issues without surrendering human judgment.

---

## Phase 4 – Pilot & Evaluation

- Limited pilot with selected teams and languages
- Compare AI-assisted and manual review
- Measure precision, recall and false-positive rates
- Test accessibility and reviewer usability
- Review bias across languages and accents
- Tune rules and decision thresholds
- Validate the employee appeal workflow

### Outcome

Evidence that the solution is useful, safe and sufficiently accurate for controlled expansion.

---

## Phase 5 – Operational Integration

- Coaching workflow
- Knowledge-base feedback loop
- Quality-management integration
- Case escalation and ownership
- Advanced trend dashboards
- Policy-rule approval workflow

### Outcome

Validated findings become structured operational improvements.

---

## Phase 6 – Scale & Continuous Governance

- Additional countries, languages and channels
- Model and rule monitoring
- Drift and quality alerts
- Periodic privacy and access reviews
- Controlled experimentation
- Continuous stakeholder feedback

### Outcome

A scalable product with measurable value and ongoing responsible-AI governance.
